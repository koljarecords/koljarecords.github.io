Hero City

Your city. Your kind of hero.

A voxel superhero game. Pick one of nine classes, design your hero, and patrol the city alone or with friends: rescue civilians, restore the power, knock out drone relays, and hold the arena against wave after wave.

  • Nine classes: fly, run, swing, freeze, burn, shock, smash, build, invent
  • A voxel workshop: Energy Builders forge hard-light shields, decoys, armour and rides; Gadget Heroes build drones, exosuits and street rovers
  • Share and trade blueprints in the player market
  • Solo or co-op on the same server-run city

Working title: Prism Forge.

Galactic Frontier

Your story. Your frontier.

A fan-made Star Wars MMO about living in the galaxy: walk the settlements of Tatooine, Naboo and Hoth, board a ship with a walkable interior, fly from the surface to orbit and into hyperspace, cross sabers, and race speeders and podracers, including Veyra, an original racing planet with five courses.

  • Planets on foot, with persistent careers and homes
  • Ships with walkable interiors, orbit and hyperspace
  • Saber combat, speeders and podracing
  • A server-run shared world, with an offline mode

Unofficial, non-commercial fan project. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Lucasfilm or Disney. Star Wars and related properties belong to their respective owners. Music by Kölja.

In-engine showcase: captured in the game, with cinematic cameras and no HUD. Music: “Galactic Frontier” by Kölja.

02 — Studio

How we work

I

Real, not faked

If a ship heels, it is because the wind pushed her. If a cannonball lands short, the sea rose under it. We build the thing, then we measure it.

II

Made by hand

Our own models, our own creatures, our own music. The Salt Meridian title theme was written and produced by Kölja.

III

Played, then argued over

Every build gets sailed, screenshotted and picked apart before it goes anywhere. What doesn't hold up gets rebuilt.
